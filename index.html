SUPPORT

Questions, answered.

Sign into your FACES account and open the Orders section. Your order status and carrier tracking information will appear when available.
FACES can support guest checkout, although creating an account makes it easier to view your order history and track future orders.
Once an order has been dispatched, its status can be updated in your FACES account and the available tracking details can be shown there.
Use the FACES AI assistant for general questions or connect the website's support contact to your customer service system before launch.
